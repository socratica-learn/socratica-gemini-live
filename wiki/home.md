## 🎯 Project Overview

Socratica is an AI-powered interactive study assistant that helps students master their subjects through:
- Interactive Socratic questioning during study sessions
- Voice-based presentation practice
- Automated note summarization
- AI-generated quizzes
- Progress tracking and analytics
- Study planning tools
